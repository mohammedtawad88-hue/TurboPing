Why MTU Matters in Gaming?

If your MTU is too large, your gaming packets get fragmented into two smaller packets. If even one piece is delayed or dropped, the game server drops your shot or causes rubber-banding. Finding the exact unfragmented MTU guarantees 100% clean packet delivery.
